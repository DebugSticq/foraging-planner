# 🌲 Foraging Planner

**Everything you need for foraging on Galatea, in one clean mod.**
Plan your Heart of the Forest, see which tree to cut next, track your Starlyn contests to the log and keep an eye on your session – without a dozen HUDs fighting for space.

> Foraging Planner **only shows information**. It never clicks, cuts, moves or plays for you.

---

## ✨ Highlights

### 📊 Session & contest tracking
- **Session card** – XP/h, coins/h, Forest Essence, tree gifts, HOTF XP and trees per minute
- **Contest card** – live points, bracket, the points you still need and a forecast for the end
- **Logs per contest** – counted from your sacks, survives a game restart mid-contest
- **Contest history & replay** – every contest with points, logs, personal bests and a point-over-time chart
- **Level forecast** – time to your next Foraging level and next pet level

### 🌳 Trees
- **Tree markers** with regrow timers and "big / small" size labels
- **Tree navigator** – an arrow, a ★ over the tree that pays the most right now, or a full route
- **Logs left to 100 %** – exactly how many logs a tree still needs
- **Precision Cutting helper**, **clean tree view** (hides flying logs) and **log-stripping protection**
- **Minimap** of the trees around you, your route and marked mobs

### 🧭 Routes
- Record your own routes with walk and **Etherwarp** points
- **Import** routes from Skyblocker, Skytils, SkyHanni, ColeWeight or plain coordinates
- **Ghost mode** – lap timer, splits and a ghost of your best lap

### 🦋 Galatea helpers
- Highlights **Hideonleaf, Shellwise, Birries, Invisibugs** and **Veilshrooms**
- **Falling mob alert** for Phanflare & Phanpyre, with a marker
- **Moonglade Beacon** – ready alert and a Tune Frequency helper
- **Stridersurfer counter**, gather rates for Sea Lumies and other sack items
- **HOTF tooltips** with the whisper cost to max and for the next 10 levels

### 🧠 Planning
- **Heart of the Forest planner** – the best perk order for your whisper budget
- **Gear advisor** – what is worth upgrading for your stats, with live Auction House and Bazaar prices
- **Goals, milestones, rare drop log, price alarms** and selling tips

### 💬 Quality of life
- **Compact chat** for tree gifts, sweep details and contest messages
- **Mute** phantoms, Drybarks, tree break sounds – or any sound with one click (`/fp sounds`)
- **Smart pause reminder** and **AFK pause** for honest per-hour numbers
- **Fully styleable HUD** – corner radius, background colour and opacity, padding, border, text shadow
- **First-start setup**: *Minimal*, *Normal* or *Everything* – change any switch later

### 🌍 Online (optional)
- **`/fp pv <player>`** – look at another player's foraging gear, HOTF, collections and pets
- **Global leaderboards** for contest points, logs per contest, XP per hour and trees per minute
  - **Off by default.** You choose whether to share.
  - Your account is confirmed through Mojang's normal server check – your login only ever goes to Mojang.
  - XP per hour and logs per contest are **measured from the Hypixel API**, not taken from the mod.

## 🚀 Getting started

1. Install **[Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/)** and **[Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)**.
2. Drop Foraging Planner into your `mods` folder.
3. Join SkyBlock and pick *Minimal*, *Normal* or *Everything*.
4. Type **`/fp help`** for all commands, or **`/fp settings`** for the settings.

[Mod Menu](https://modrinth.com/mod/modmenu) is supported but not required.

## ⌨️ Most used commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/fp settings` | Open the settings |
| `/fp stats` | Statistics: goals, sessions, contests, drops, routes, chapters |
| `/fp plan` | Heart of the Forest plan |
| `/fp gear` | Gear advisor |
| `/fp routes` | Record, import and manage routes |
| `/fp pv <player>` | View another player |

## ❓ FAQ

**Is this allowed on Hypixel?**
Foraging Planner is a display mod: it reads what the game already shows you (chat, scoreboard, tab list, menus) and presents it differently. It does not automate any input. As with every mod, use it at your own risk and follow the [Hypixel rules](https://hypixel.net/rules).

**Does it send my data anywhere?**
Only if you use the online features. `/fp pv` asks the mod's own server, which talks to the Hypixel API. The leaderboards are off until you turn them on, and turning them off removes your entries.

**Which Minecraft version?**
Minecraft **26.3** with Fabric.
